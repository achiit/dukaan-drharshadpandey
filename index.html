Ask a question
What is a dental implant?

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. A crown, bridge or denture is then fixed on it. Dr. Pandey will check if you are a suitable candidate.

Implant or denture: which one is right for me?

Implants and fixed teeth feel closer to natural teeth. Dentures are a simpler option for many people. The right choice depends on your gums, bone and budget, and Dr. Pandey will explain both after examination.

Is a root canal painful?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Mild tenderness for a day or two afterwards can happen. A root canal usually saves a tooth you might otherwise lose.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Many people benefit from scaling and polishing about once every six months. Ask the doctor what is right for your gums.

Do I need an appointment?

You can walk in during clinic hours, 10:00 AM to 8:30 PM, any day. Booking on WhatsApp first helps reduce your waiting time.

I have a severe toothache or swelling. What should I do?

Call the clinic right away on either number below. Do not wait for the pain to pass, as swelling and infection can get worse.
